## Part 2 — statement-back of the frozen rules (in my own words, before writing the scorer)
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00  SOURCE: prereg_frozen_v0.3.md as committed at 06a3e70 (SHA-256 d09de709...8f55e9). The prereg is authoritative; where the task brief and the prereg differ, the prereg wins. I have not modified it.
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00  
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00  **THE UNIT.** A claim = one token in one lens's top-k list at one (item, position, layer). Precision is over claims, not items. Five items therefore give hundreds of claims.
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00  
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00  **THE FOUR BUCKETS.** Every claim lands in exactly one:
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00    (1) TP -- true positive. The claim token is a registered surface form of the country that the ITEM ITSELF is expected to resolve to (the country the model gave at >=4/5 in elicitation). Only the working condition can produce a TP: a twin has no correct country by construction, and a bare mention has no question attached.
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00    (2) FP-P -- false positive, paired. A country claim on that item's BROKEN TWIN. Per section 4a the FP unit here is a *commit*, not a bare appearance: on an empty-search twin (sandal, 83rd parallel, Spanish in East Asia, Danish in S. America) it is a commit to ANY country; on a forced-choice twin (Spanish in S. America, French in S. America) it is a commit to a SINGLE country while the model's own behaviour scatters. A twin country claim that never reaches commit is a smear, and a smear is the twin behaving CORRECTLY -- so it is not an FP.
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00    (3) FP-C -- false positive, cross. The claim token is a registered form of a DIFFERENT item's expected country (Brazil tokens on the Korea item; Italy tokens on any confirmatory item). This is the stricter test and the prereg makes it primary.
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00    (4) UNSCORED -- none of the above. Everything the fragment filter rejects, plus every token that is in no registry at all (' heel', 'soles', '-shaped'). The prereg is explicit that ambiguous tokens are NEVER forced into TP or FP, so UNSCORED is the default, not a failure state.
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00    Precision = TP / (TP + FP), where FP = FP-P + FP-C. UNSCORED claims are in neither numerator nor denominator. Computed per layer x lens x condition; per-layer is primary, best-rank-in-band secondary.
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00  
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00  **THE COMMIT RULE.** A lens commits to a country at a position iff ANY registered form of that country appears at rank <= 3 for at least 3 CONSECUTIVE layers inside the analysis band L8-20. The forms are allowed to differ from layer to layer -- a streak carried by '意大利' at one layer and ' Ital' at the next still counts as one streak, because the claim is about the country, not the spelling. The fragment filter is applied before ranks are read. Anything weaker than that -- rank 4, or only two layers in a row -- is a smear, not a commit. Nothing about the commit rule is mine to tune: rank<=3, run length 3, band 8-20 are all frozen.
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00  
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00  **HOW THE ENTITY POSITION IS FOUND, PER PROMPT.** The pilot found a division of labour: the intermediate answer (the country) shows up at the ENTITY position = the final token of the description, while the downstream answer (the currency/capital) shows up at the final prompt position. So the country is scored at the entity position and the downstream answer, where the item has one, at the final position, reported separately.
- 2026-09-01T14:11:56+00:00    Operationally, for each prompt I declare the description's last WORD (its 'anchor'), and the entity position is the index of that word's final SUB-token -- found the way the pilot's readout_boot_sandal.py found it: decode every token, walk all spans, and take the last index of the span whose concatenated text strips to the anchor.
